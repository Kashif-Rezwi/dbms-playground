**Track:** MongoDB · **Difficulty:** Advanced · Full spec: **[../projects/08-capstone.md](../projects/08-capstone.md)**

## The Assignment (short version)

**Build "FitTrack"** — a fitness-tracking app's MongoDB backend — designed and built by you, with the *reasoning* as the deliverable:

1. **Access patterns first** (Day 12's workflow): list the app's top ~8 reads/writes ranked
2. **Schema design** with the embed/reference framework + at least three of the seven named patterns used *deliberately and labeled*
3. **Indexes** — ESR-reasoned, ratio-verified, ≤5
4. **Transactions** — exactly where multi-doc invariants demand them (and NOT where embedding already made things atomic — label the distinction)
5. **Shape contract** — validators + the audit queries
6. **Production memo** — replica set, write concerns for critical writes, backups, the working-set math, and your honest "should this even be MongoDB?" paragraph
7. **Defense** — the recorded interview (see the full spec)

## Suggested Split

**Day 29** — access patterns → schema → validators → seed data → indexes.
**Day 30** — queries/pipelines → the transaction flow → performance numbers → the production memo → the recorded defense.
